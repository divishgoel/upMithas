Heritage

From our farm to your kitchen.

Rooted in the fertile soil of Uttar Pradesh, our family has cultivated sugarcane for generations. UP Mithas is not just a brand; it is the continuation of an ancestral legacy, preserving the pure, unrefined sweetness of traditional jaggery.

eco

Our mission is simple: to offer a healthy, traditional alternative to refined sugar, crafted without a single chemical intervention.

The Art of Sweetness

We believe that true flavor cannot be rushed by industrial machinery. Our process relies on time, temperature, and generations of intuitive knowledge.

1. The Harvest & Crush

Selected canes are harvested at peak ripeness and immediately crushed to extract the vital juice, ensuring no loss of natural nutrients.

2. The Kadhai Boil

Slowly simmered in traditional iron pans, the juice naturally clarifies. We rely solely on the heat of the fire and the skill of our artisans to reach the perfect consistency.

volunteer_activism

Sustainability & Fair Trade

We work hand-in-hand with generational farming families across Uttar Pradesh, paying fair, above-market rates and reinvesting in soil health so that this tradition endures for generations to come.
